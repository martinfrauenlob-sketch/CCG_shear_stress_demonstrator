Shear stress & velocity distribution

Interactive microfluidic channel model · adjust the inputs to see the flow velocity profile across the channel height and the corresponding shear-stress magnitude shown in green.

Wide rectangular channel
Mean velocity: U = Q / (w h)
Velocity profile: u(y) = 6U (y/h)(1 − y/h)
Shear-stress magnitude: |τ(y)| = τw|1 − 2y/h|
Wall shear: τw ≈ 6μU/h

Assumptions: Newtonian fluid, pressure-driven, fully developed laminar flow, and a channel much wider than its height. The blue curve is the parabolic velocity distribution. The green line is the shear-stress magnitude, shown on the top x-axis. Both x-axes are fixed across the full slider range so all possible profiles remain visible.
